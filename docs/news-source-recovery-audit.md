# Historical News Source Recovery Audit

This register tracks approved historical reports that must not receive a citation until their underlying claim is corrected or independently verified. A missing source is not permission to infer or invent one.

## Classifications

- `correction_required`: an authoritative source contradicts a material claim in the report.
- `internally_sourced`: the report presents Albaalaagh's own reporting or confidential sources and no public original source proves the exclusive claim.
- `unverified`: no sufficiently reliable, contemporaneous source has been found.
- `verified`: a source has been added to `news_citations` and attribution appears inside the article body.
